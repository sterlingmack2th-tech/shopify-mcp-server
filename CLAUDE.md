# Claude Rules for This Project

## Email Rules (PERMANENT)

NEVER use dashes of any kind in emails. This means:
- No em dashes
- No hyphens used as dashes
- No bullet points using dashes
- No en dashes

This applies to every email drafted or sent through Gmail, no exceptions. Dashes in emails look AI-generated and cause outreach to fail.

## Security Constraint

Never send, reply to, forward, delete, archive, or modify an email unless Sterling explicitly authorizes it.

## Draft First Rule

Always draft emails and show them to Sterling for approval before sending. Never send without explicit approval.
